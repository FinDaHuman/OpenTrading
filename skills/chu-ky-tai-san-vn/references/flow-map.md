# Vòng luân chuyển tiền VN (logic, số tra tool)

Cheap money/SBV easing (Reflation) → government bonds (yields falling) + low savings
→ money leaves deposits → stocks when E/P − the deposit rate > 0 (Recovery)
→ hot stocks + tight USD + a widening SJC spread (Overheat: gold/USD/real estate)
→ the corporate-bond wall + margin stress + the basis flipping to a premium (distribution)
→ recession → savings/USD/gold (shelter)

Quantitative triggers (verify with tools each time):
- A hot monthly USD rise + foreign net selling = reduce the equity weight (the VNI–USD correlation: look it up in the tool; never use a remembered number).
- SJC spread > 10% = speculation/imminent intervention (timeline in the anchors).
- A deep basis discount + high liquidity = a bottom zone; a premium + euphoria = the end of a wave.
- A strong BTC month = an early risk-on signal for VNI (check in `vn_asset_chains`); gold/USD are not used for forecasting.
- Real-estate corporate bond coupons > 10% + a Q4 maturity wall = systemic risk hanging over real estate/banks.
