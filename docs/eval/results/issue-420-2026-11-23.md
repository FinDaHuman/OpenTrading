## Compact then continue

Each history had four turns; the first two were summarized and the final two kept verbatim. We entered `/compact`, exited the TUI, restarted with `-c`, and asked for the first question and the value in the second answer without calling tools. The replay used a copy of the exact original pre-compaction session and the changed prompt/marker; it was re-compacted and resumed in the real TUI.

No tools were called after resume in these four runs. The financial summary also placed the fictional figures in its verified-figures table and attributed them to a previous answer, while elsewhere calling the data fictional; the original input was user-provided. The change does not establish correct source attribution. These are two paired samples on one model, not a guarantee about other prompts or models.
